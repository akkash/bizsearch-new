# Homepage Override — BizSearch Marketplace

Overrides MASTER for the public homepage. Goal: transaction start, not brochure.

## Information Architecture (strict order)

1. Navigation
2. Primary search / discovery (hero)
3. Popular searches / quick start + three user paths
4. Matched business opportunities
5. Featured franchise opportunities
6. Understand before you buy (valuation / BI preview)
7. Trust / verification
8. Sell your business
9. Final CTA
10. Footer

## Do not include

- Generic AI feature grids
- Fake stats / fake testimonials / fake logos
- Repeated CTA bands
- Franchise-only framing as the whole product

## Hero copy

- Headline: "Find a business worth buying."
- Support: "Search businesses and franchises for sale by location, industry, investment, revenue and more."
- Search placeholder: "Try: Restaurant in Chennai under ₹75L"
- Primary focal point: search box

## Three paths (visible above fold or immediately below search)

- Buy a Business → `/businesses`
- Find a Franchise → `/franchises`
- Sell a Business → `/add-business-listing`

## Demo data rule

Any illustrative valuation or match preview must be labeled **Sample analysis** — never presented as live marketplace activity.
